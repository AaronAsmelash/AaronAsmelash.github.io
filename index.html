04 · How I work

One person across the test lifecycle.

I write the test strategy, build the automation, wire it into CI, and report what it found. The same person who designs a suite runs it in the pipeline and explains the result to the programme team.

The work is measured. I track script build time, manual effort removed, execution time and defect trends, and I report the figures rather than adjectives.

I have worked in Agile Scrum teams from daily stand-ups through sprint demos, and I am comfortable with Waterfall programmes when a project needs one.
